### What makes a great listing title?[^1]

#### Overview
- clear, easy-to-scan
- focus on clarity and relevance in your listing titles, while moving other key details to listing tags, descriptions, and attributes.

#### Principles
- **Clearly state the item for sale**, like “mug” or “dress”
- **Include the most important traits upfront**, like your item’s color, material, and size
- **Streamline for clarity and scanability**. Consider using less than *15 words* when including the traits that make your item special.
- **Only include holidays, special occasions, or recipient types if they’re essential to what the item is**, like “birthday candle” or “Halloween costume”
- **Move subjective words, like “perfect” or “beautiful,” to the description**
- **Try not to repeat words**
- **Don’t worry about adding info about price, shipping, or sales.** Etsy helps take care of this by badging your items in Search.

#### Guidance

| Listing title trait or keyword type           | Examples                                          | Keep in title? | What to do                                                                                                                                              |
| --------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------- | -------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Noun                                          | dress, mug, table                                 | Yes            | Clearly state what the item is just once in the listing title                                                                                           |
| Objective descriptors                         | neon pink, extra large, reclaimed wood, vintage   | Yes            | Include the top three most critical descriptors for the category (color, material, and size) as well as other key information that sets your item apart |
| Holiday-related words                         | Birthday candle, Halloween costume                | Sometimes      | Only include holidays, special occasions, or recipient types if they’re essential to what the item is                                                   |
| Aspirational and gifting keywords and phrases | gift for him, birthday present, personalized gift | No             | Avoid repetition of aspirational or gifting keywords and phrases                                                                                        |
| Subjective descriptors                        | beautiful, perfect, wonderful                     | No             | Move to tags or attributes                                                                                                                              |
| How the item is sold                          | on sale, free shipping                            | No             | Remove completely from listing                                                                                                                          |
| Unnecessary repeated words or phrases         | unisex adult poncho, unisex cotton poncho         | No             | Remove repeated words or phrases                                                                                                                        |
