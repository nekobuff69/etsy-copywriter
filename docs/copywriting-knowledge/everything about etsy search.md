# 搜索流程
### **Query matching:**
A buyer enters a query and we look at the inventory of listings that match that query.

Using **all 13 tags**, adding variety to your tags, adding all the relevant attribute options, and categorizing your items as specifically as you can may all help increase the chance that your listings will match with a query.

Diversifying your inventory and trying lots of different relevant tags on your listings gives you the opportunity to match with more searches.

## **Ranking:**
Behind the scenes, we rank the matching listings based on a variety of factors, which we’ll talk about in more detail below.

Here are some of the factors that go into Etsy search ranking:

1. *Relevancy*
When your tags, titles, listing descriptions, categories, and attributes match a shopper’s query, your listings have the potential to appear in their search results. *If those keywords are an exact match for the query, your listing may be ranked higher in results because it’s seen as more relevant to the search.*
(用常见词，预估买家输入搜索栏的关键字)

2. *Shop quality*
Buyers want to know who they’re purchasing from, which is important when building trust in your shop and building your brand. For example, your shop should have a shop icon, which helps to represent your business. 
(店铺装修要专业，得体)

3. *Listing quality*
When shopping from the millions of unique items on Etsy, we want to make sure high quality listings with clear information stand out so that buyers feel confident in making a purchase.
*最基本要包含高质量产品照，退货条款*

4. [*Customer Service Quality*](https://www.etsy.com/seller-handbook/article/375461267511)
Shops that consistently follow our customer service standards are prioritized in search. Considered factors:
- average review ratings
- 回复信息的效率
- 单次服务的评分
（以最近三个月的数据计算）

4. *Translations and language*
 - Choose a shop language when setting up your Etsy shop.  
- All titles, tags, and listing descriptions must be written in the selected language.  
- Etsy will translate your keywords to match shoppers’ query and language settings. 

 Also, we can add custom translation.  Get step by step instructions for adding your own translation in this [Help article](https://help.etsy.com/hc/en-us/articles/360000343048).

5. *Listing Engagement Rate*
- Conversion rate measures the proportion of viewers who purchase, indicating buyer interest and boosting engagement.  
- A listing’s conversion rate directly affects its search ranking on Etsy.  
- During vacation mode, traffic may drop because Etsy gathers new data on buyer interactions while engagement rates remain unchanged.  
- After returning from vacation mode, Etsy resumes collecting current interaction data to update rankings.  

Learn more about how conversion rate influences search ranking in [Create Listings That Convert](https://www.etsy.com/seller-handbook/article/366469719354).

6. *Recency*
- New listings receive a short‑term search boost to gauge buyer interaction and calculate engagement rates.  
- Renewed listings get a smaller, similar temporary boost.  
- The boost duration varies from hours to days based on search frequency for the item.  
- Using renewals or new listings solely for this boost is not an effective SEO tactic.  
- Focus instead on improving tags and boosting conversion rates for better long‑term visibility.

6. *Shopper's habbits*
- Etsy’s Context Specific Ranking customizes search results by learning buyers’ interests to show the most likely purchase items.  
- The search experience varies per buyer; a seller’s own searches don’t reveal where their listings actually appear in broader search results.  
- Sellers should use shop stats, especially the traffic details page, to identify which listings receive the most Etsy‑search traffic and the terms driving that traffic.  
- Compare low‑view listings’ tags with those of high‑performing ones; consider refreshing tags or adding popular keywords.  
- Optimize underperforming listings for conversion by following guidance such as  [Create Listings That Convert](https://www.etsy.com/seller-handbook/article/366469719354).
# 注意
Before we get into the details, a few important details to note:
- Subscribing to Etsy Plus does not influence where listings appear in Etsy search.
- [Star Seller](https://www.etsy.com/starseller) does not directly impact the ordering of search results, however it does serve as a further trust signal to buyers and can drive increased conversion for your shop.
- Running an Etsy Ads campaign does not influence where your listings appear in Etsy search outside of the designated advertising spaces in search results. Learn more about Etsy’s advertising policy [here](https://www.etsy.com/legal/advertising/).
