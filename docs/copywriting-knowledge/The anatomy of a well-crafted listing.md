In this article, we’ll break down the anatomy of an Etsy listing page, helping you understand key elements and offering tips to optimize them for maximum visibility, engagement, and sales. While the specifics of how your listing is displayed can vary depending on how a shopper is viewing it—whether on desktop, mobile, or through Etsy’s app—the core elements that make up a strong listing remain the same. Let's dive in!

### 1. Listing Images

-   The first listing image is crucial for attracting clicks from search results; subsequent images help close sales.
-   To optimize the first thumbnail for SEO, **avoid text overlays, collages, or using multiple images as the initial thumbnail. **
-   You can upload **up to 20 photos per listing**.
-   Adding more images generally boosts conversion rates by providing shoppers with additional product details.
-   Example: art buyers may want a styled room photo to gauge size and style; jewelry shoppers may prefer a model wearing the piece.

**Key tips:**

-   Capture multiple angles, including close‑ups that emphasize details and textures.
-   Include lifestyle images showing the product in use or within its intended environment.
-   Use natural lighting; avoid flash to prevent harsh shadows and preserve accurate colors.
-   Keep the background simple and neutral so the focus remains on the product.
-   Ensure high resolution: at least 635 × 635 pixels for basic listings, but aim for 2000 × 2000 pixels or more for optimal visual impact.

Check out the [7 Essential Types of Product Photos](https://www.etsy.com/seller-handbook/article/7-essential-types-of-product-photos/22504064051) to learn more.

### 2. Listing Titles

-   Titles influence shopper clicks by clearly showing what’s being sold and its unique features.
-   Only the first few words of a title appear in search results, so use that space to describe the item.
-   Keep titles descriptive yet concise and easy to read.
-   Etsy search evaluates the entire listing (title, tags, attributes, description, photo, reviews) to match buyers.
-   The title should focus on clarity and relevance; other details can be placed in tags, descriptions, and attributes for better visibility and buyer confidence.

-   Name the item clearly (e.g., “mug” or “dress”).
-   List the most important traits first: color, material, size.
-   Keep trait descriptions concise—aim for under 15 words to aid scannability.
-   Include holidays or recipient types only if they define the item (“birthday candle,” “Halloween costume”).
-   Reserve subjective adjectives (e.g., “perfect,” “beautiful”) for the description section.
-   Avoid repeating words throughout the title.
-   Omit price, shipping, and sales details; Etsy handles those in Search.

### 3. Price

-   A finished product’s cost includes all expenses from creation, sale, and shipment.
-   Understanding these costs plus desired profit helps set a base retail price.
-   Experimentation and market research refine pricing over time.
-   Items should be priced to remain profitable yet competitive; high shipping fees often deter buyers.
-   Shifting some shipping costs into the item price while keeping the total cost unchanged can boost sales.

Check out [How to Price for Profit, Conversion, and Growth](https://www.etsy.com/seller-handbook/article/1106022743419#Pricing) to learn more about two popular approaches to pricing. Plus, get a refresher on [How Shipping Price Is Factored Into Search for US Domestic Listings](https://www.etsy.com/seller-handbook/article/1293023712519).

### 4. Estimated delivery date

-   Quick delivery is a key shopper priority.
-   Provide order progress updates throughout the purchase experience.
-   Share processing times and shipping service details (mail class, carrier).
-   Adjust stated processing times in Shipping Profiles to set realistic buyer expectations.
-   Ensure accuracy about preparation and shipment coordination times.

### 5. Shop policies

Clear shop policies let buyers know what they can expect when they make a purchase from your shop, like whether you’ll accept returns or exchanges. **Having shop policies has a positive effect on your search placement and using Etsy’s shop policies template can give you an additional boost.** Learn more about customer and market experience score and its influence on search results in [How Great Customer Service Can Improve Your Search Ranking](https://www.etsy.com/seller-handbook/article/375461267511).

### 6. Listing description

-   Keep listing descriptions short, informative, yet engaging
-   Place essential details first (sizes, dimensions, colors, ordering directions)
-   End with a compelling narrative that highlights the product’s story and brand identity
-   Etsy search ranking uses keywords and phrases in descriptions as part of its second‑phase algorithm.

**Key tips:**

-   Incorporate relevant keywords early in the description, but avoid copying the title verbatim.
-   Craft a natural, brand‑voice sentence that casually weaves top keywords without sounding forced.
-   Provide clear, engaging details: product uniqueness, dimensions, materials, care instructions, and customization options.
-   Share a brief story about the inspiration or creation process to connect with customers.
-   Use bullet points or short paragraphs for readability and to prevent intimidating long blocks of text.

### 7. Average review rating

**Star ratings of 4 or 5** help attract new buyers and keep them coming back to your shop. For more tips, read [How to Get 5-Star Reviews](https://www.etsy.com/seller-handbook/article/476775452989).

**Note**: New listings will still be shown in search even if they don’t have any reviews or ratings yet, as long as they don’t have any other listing or shop quality issues. Learn more about [search visibility on Etsy](https://www.etsy.com/seller-handbook/article/1289139008351).

**Bonus: Build buyer trust with a Star Seller badge**
Consistently going above and beyond for your customers could also help you become a [Star Seller](https://www.etsy.com/seller-handbook/article/1020956976086), which serves as a further trust signal to buyers and can drive increased conversion for your shop. You can keep track of your daily progress on your [customer service stats page](https://etsy.me/service-stats).

### 8. Attributes

-   Attributes act like tags and improve matching with shopper searches.
-   They describe specific characteristics such as material, primary/secondary color, holiday, occasion, height, and width.
-   Adding more detailed categories allows you to include unique attributes for that category (e.g., chain style or number of strands for necklaces; bead size and shape for craft supplies).
-   Choose the attribute option that best fits your item, even if it differs from how you’d normally describe it.
-   Super‑specific keywords in attributes help shoppers find items precisely, eliminating the need to duplicate them as separate tags.
    [Try adding attributes.](https://www.etsy.com/your/shops/me/tools/listings)

### 9. Tags

-   Each tag on a listing increases its visibility in shopper searches; unused or irrelevant tags represent missed opportunities.
-   Aim to add **13 tags** to every listing.
-   Tags should be multi‑word phrases written in natural, conversational language.
-   A good rule of thumb: **if you can’t picture someone typing the phrase into Google, it probably shouldn’t be a tag.**
-   Include variety by highlighting what the product is, how it’s made, and who it’s intended for.

### 10. You and your small business story

> Jewelry designer Jessica Little uses her [about section](https://www.etsy.com/shop/JessALittleShop#about) to share the origins of her shop, along with her business ethos and a nod to her target audience.

-   Etsy shoppers value the personal connection to real people behind products, boosting purchase confidence and brand loyalty.
-   Adding photos and details about your brand story in the shop’s about section lets buyers peek behind the scenes of your passion and process.
-   A compelling About page can spark unexpected opportunities such as press coverage, collaborations, and new business prospects.
-   Your shop title is another storytelling channel that influences SEO; use it to convey a tagline or concise description of what you sell.

Check out [How to Craft a Killer About Section](https://www.etsy.com/seller-handbook/article/22636178725) for more inspiration.
