## Essentials

-   Keywords across titles, descriptions, tags, categories, and attributes are crucial for matching listings with buyers.
-   Etsy search compiles all listings containing keywords that match a shopper’s query.
-   Listings are then ranked so shoppers see the most relevant items first.
-   Using strong, relevant keywords increases the chance of your items appearing in search results.
-   Follow best‑practice tips to maximize keyword effectiveness in your shop.

## Tips

### Categories

-   Use the most specific subcategory available for each item to maximize search visibility.
-   Adding a specific subcategory automatically includes the item in all parent categories of that subcategory.
-   Treat categories as tags; avoid duplicating category phrases with separate tags.
-   Example: Listing under “Nail Stencils” will appear in searches for “nail stencils,” and also in broader categories like “Craft Supplies” and “Makeup & Cosmetics.”

### Attributes

-   Attributes are extra details you add after choosing a category—general ones like color or material, plus category‑specific ones such as pattern for clothing or room for home décor.
-   Each attribute acts like a tag, helping your listing surface in shoppers’ searches that use those terms.
-   Add _all_ relevant options, even if they’re less precise:
    -   If you describe yarn as “magenta,” also add the color attribute “pink” because it’s accurate and broadens search visibility.
    -   If a t‑shirt pattern is described as “nature inspired,” don’t miss adding the specific Plants & trees pattern attribute; this captures shoppers searching that exact term.
-   After setting attributes, use your listing description, title, and tags to describe the item in your own words.
-   You don’t need duplicate tags for attributes you’ve already added:
    -   For example, if a rug has the "Faux fur" attribute, skip “faux fur” as a separate tag; instead include it within a multi‑word descriptive tag like “faux fur accent rug.”

Learn more about attributes in [Add Attributes to Help Increase Your Shop's Visibility](https://www.etsy.com/seller-handbook/article/604203126614).

### Titles

**Related note: [[如何撰写商品标题]]**

-   Keywords in titles help match shopper queries but are only one factor in Etsy’s holistic search algorithm.
-   Etsy evaluates all listing data—title, tags, attributes, description, first photo, reviews, etc.—to connect items with buyers.
-   Write short, clear, descriptive titles that quickly convey what you’re selling, especially on mobile where shoppers scan fast.
-   Lead the title with the most relevant keywords; the order of phrases doesn’t affect ranking, and punctuation or symbols can separate phrases without hurting searchability.
-   Focus on buyer-friendly language rather than optimizing solely for the algorithm.
-   Avoid long keyword lists that may confuse or deter buyers.
-   Ensure all descriptors are accurate and comply with Etsy’s policies to prevent misleading listings (e.g., don’t label non‑cashmere items as “cashmere”).

### Listing Descriptions

Etsy search considers keywords and phrases within your listing descriptions when ranking your listings. The keywords you use across your listing titles, descriptions, tags, categories, and attributes are essential when it comes to query matching, the first phase of search ranking, within Etsy’s search algorithm.

### Tags

**Related note: [[The anatomy of a well-crafted listing#9. Tags]]**

-   Max: 13 tags

#### Dos & Donts

**Dos**

-   Use all 13 tags on each listing to maximize search visibility.
-   Prefer multi‑word phrases (up to 20 characters) over single words; e.g., “custom bracelet” beats separate “custom” and “bracelet.”
-   Regularly review Shop Stats, refresh underperforming tags, and diversify your keyword set.
-   Include synonyms and regional spellings/phrases that shoppers might use:
    -   If shoppers search for the British spelling “jewellery,” still tag with “Jewelry” so both variations appear.
    -   For Australian customers looking for flip‑flops, add the phrase “thong sandals.”
-   Focus on long‑tail keywords that highlight unique product features rather than generic terms (“canvas tote bag” vs. “tote bag”).
    ![[Pasted image 20260130202337.png]]
    > The y axis of this chart is the number of times a term is searched and the x axis is conversion rate for those searches. Long tail keywords are the descriptive, multi-word terms that would appear on the right side of the graph—the one that looks like a long tail. Although they may be used less often than more popular phrases (those on the left side of the chart) they tend to convert better because they describe exactly what a shopper is looking for.

**Donts**
- **Avoid repeating tags** – use 13 distinct tags to reach more buyers (e.g., “octopus art print” and “animal wall decor” instead of “octopus art” and “octopus print”).  
- **Don’t duplicate categories or attributes as tags** – if your item is in the *Statement Ring* category, you don’t need a separate tag for “statement ring.”  
- **Do not include misspellings** – Etsy’s search auto‑corrects common typos, so purposely misspelling keywords (e.g., “octopuss art”) won’t help.  
- **Keep tags in one language** – titles and tags should match the shop’s chosen language; you can translate them yourself if you speak multiple languages.  
- **Plurals are handled automatically** – a search for “diaries” will still match listings tagged with “diary,” so separate plural tags aren’t necessary.
